## 🔴 Priority 1 — Free, Do This Week

### 1. Google Business Profile (Most Important)
- Go to **business.google.com** and claim/create your listing
- Category: "Health Spa" + "Sauna" + "Wellness Center"
- Add all photos, hours, description, website link
- This alone can put you on Google Maps top 3 for "sauna Chiang Mai"

### 2. Submit to Google Search Console
- Go to **search.google.com/search-console**
- Add property: https://www.fermentspace.com
- Submit your sitemap: https://www.fermentspace.com/sitemap.xml
- Request indexing of your homepage

### 3. Submit sitemap to Bing
- Go to **bing.com/webmasters**
- Submit sitemap URL

### 4. Social Profile Links (easy backlinks)

- Facebook page → add website URL ✅ (do now)
